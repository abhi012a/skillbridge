🚀 **Excited to share SkillBridge: A platform we built to help students discover and track career opportunities!** 🎓⚡

As college students, we’ve all been there:
You find out about an amazing hackathon, a Google/Microsoft internship opening, or a high-value scholarship... but the deadline passed three days ago. Or it was buried under 500 messages in a college WhatsApp group or Discord server.

We got tired of this constant FOMO and fragmentation, so we built **SkillBridge** — a clean, student-first platform where you can discover and track verified opportunities in one place.

🔥 **What we built into SkillBridge:**
1. **🎓 Personalized Student Profile**: Add your major, skills (Python, React, Cloud, ML, etc.), and career interests.
2. **🎯 Transparent Skill Matching**: Get a clear 0–100% match score for every opportunity with an explanation of why it fits and what skills you can learn next.
3. **🔎 Instant Multi-Category Filters**: Browse Hackathons, Internships, Scholarships, Certifications, and Workshops by mode (Remote, Hybrid, In-Person) and fee (100% Free).
4. **📋 Application Tracker**: Move applications across stages (*Saved → Applied → Interviewing → Accepted*) with personal notes and reminders.
5. **⏰ Approaching Deadlines & Calendar Sync**: 1-click export to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar (.ics) and an urgent 90-day deadline radar.
6. **💡 In-Demand Skills Radar**: See which skills are trending across active listings so you know what to learn next.
